# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards of Behavior

Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and respect toward other community members.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Unacceptable behaviors:
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information of others without explicit consent.
- Other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement and Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate corrective action against inappropriate conduct.

Community leaders maintain the authority to remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting and Contact

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to community maintainers at `dafagareth@gmail.com`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and impartially.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and confidentiality of the reporter.
